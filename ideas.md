# Restart Inc Positioning Report — Design Direction

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Operator's Field Notes
Very Brief Intro: An editorial strategy dossier that makes complex technology positioning feel clear, credible, and human. Warm paper tones, graphite, and signal orange create the feeling of a trusted briefing prepared for decision-makers.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Night Operations Console
Very Brief Intro: A dark, high-contrast command-center interface for scanning market signals, risks, and recommended moves. Used sparingly because it can over-index on technical theater.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Lagos Modernist
Very Brief Intro: A bright, optimistic information design system inspired by West African editorial design, with confident blocks of color and a precise, civic tone. It frames Restart as a visible institution, not only a delivery team.
Probability: 0.08

## Chosen direction: Operator's Field Notes

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial modernism with the rigor of a strategy consulting brief and the warmth of a well-used field notebook.

### Core Principles
1. Evidence before hype: show the source, the implication, and the action.
2. Senior clarity: use strong hierarchy and plainspoken language instead of jargon walls.
3. Trust as a visible system: make reliability, privacy, delivery discipline, and stewardship feel tangible.
4. Useful motion: interactions reveal context and comparisons without turning research into a toy.

### Color Philosophy
The base is warm mineral paper (#F3F0E8) and ink-black graphite (#1B211F) to create calm, credible reading conditions. Signal orange (#F26A3D) marks decisions and opportunity; desaturated teal (#2B6F6B) marks trust, controls, and evidence. This is intentionally not a glossy SaaS palette: the report should feel considered, durable, and operational.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored editorial rail keeps section labels and source provenance visible while the right canvas carries the argument. Large asymmetric panels alternate with compact evidence cards. Data visualizations are treated as annotated exhibits, not dashboard widgets.

### Signature Elements
- Numbered exhibit labels with source tags, inspired by analyst workpapers.
- A thin orange “signal line” that connects evidence to recommended action.
- Quiet paper grain and low-contrast ruled lines that add depth without distracting from the content.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should answer a reader question: what changed, why it matters, or what to do next. Tabs switch between “Current signal” and “Strategic move”; chart hover reveals source notes; copy/share controls make the report portable.

### Animation
Use subtle 180–240ms ease-out reveals for cards and chart annotations. Animate only opacity and transform. Charts should enter with a short draw-up effect, while hover states should be immediate enough to feel analytical. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use DM Serif Display for the report title and major thesis statements, paired with Plus Jakarta Sans for body copy, labels, and UI. Headlines are sentence case, high-contrast, and short. Body copy is 15–17px with generous line height. Metadata uses uppercase 10–11px labels with tracking.

### Brand Essence
Restart is the senior technology partner that turns ambitious digital products into dependable growth systems for serious operators. Personality: grounded, exacting, generous.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound decisive and human; CTAs sound like a thoughtful next step, not a sales funnel. Microcopy names the evidence and the tradeoff.
Example lines: “Make dependable delivery the product.” / “Show the system behind the promise.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact “R/” monogram: a vertical graphite stem with a single orange diagonal signal stroke, suggesting restart, forward motion, and a clean handoff from strategy to execution. No default text logo treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
Signal Orange — #F26A3D. It is the color of the next move: visible, warm, and ownable without looking like a generic tech accent.
